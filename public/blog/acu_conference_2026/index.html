ZhenChiKalpa to Participate as Delegate at ACUCON 2026

ZhenChiKalpa to Participate as Delegate at ACUCON 2026

We are happy to share that ZhenChiKalpa has officially registered as a delegate for ACUCON 2026 and travel arrangements have already been completed.

ACUCON 2026, scheduled for 11–12 July 2026 in Nagpur, brings together practitioners, educational sessions, workshops, and professional interaction within the acupuncture community.

At ZhenChiKalpa, we believe that continuous learning, professional exchange, and active participation contribute to long-term growth in healthcare practice.

Participating in professional events creates opportunities to:

  • Learn from educational sessions and discussions
  • Interact with fellow practitioners
  • Observe different perspectives and approaches
  • Stay connected with developments in acupuncture education
  • Reflect and improve continuously

Healthcare ecosystems grow through the collective efforts of practitioners, clinics, educational institutions, and communities.

As a participating delegate, we look forward to attending, learning, and sharing selected reflections after the conference.

Visitors interested in learning more about the conference can visit the official website:

Official Conference Website: https://acucon.ayushmanacupuncture.com/

We look forward to bringing back insights and sharing our experience with the ZhenChiKalpa community after the event.

— Team ZhenChiKalpa
